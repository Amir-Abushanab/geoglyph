Add `ASEAN`, the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, drawn like `EU`: its 11 member
states, Timor-Leste included since October 2025, each cropped as its own glyph is and
dissolved into one outline, with flag-icons' `asean.svg` in both tiers. It is in `CODES`,
`load`, `has` and the `shape/`, `flag/` and `flag-px/` subpaths like any country, the first
code longer than two letters.

Add `EAC`, the East African Community, the same way: its 8 partner states, Somalia and the
DR Congo included, with flag-icons' `eac.svg`. Somalia is what Natural Earth files under
`SO`, so it is drawn without Somaliland, as the `SO` glyph is.

Add `ARAB`, the League of Arab States: its 22 members, Syria included, with flag-icons'
`arab.svg`. Morocco is drawn without Western Sahara and Somalia without Somaliland, as their
own glyphs are, and Palestine as its own glyph is. The Comoros stays though it is far from
every other member: a member of a bloc is now never cropped out of it for distance, as it
was never dropped for size.
