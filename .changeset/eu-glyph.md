Add `EU`, the European Union: the one code that is not a country, reserved for the Union by
ISO 3166 and drawn by flag-icons. Its shape is the 27 member states, each cropped as its own
glyph is (France without its overseas regions, Portugal without the Azores and Madeira,
Spain without the Canaries, Åland with Finland), dissolved into one outline so no frontier
between them is drawn. Switzerland stays a hole, and every member stays in, Malta included.
Its flag is flag-icons' `eu.svg` in both tiers, and it is in `CODES`, `load`, `has` and the
`shape/`, `flag/` and `flag-px/` subpaths like any country.
